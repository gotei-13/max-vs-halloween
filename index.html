How to play
Walkerfootsteps on the floor, from any side
Flyerwhoosh and "oooooh" from above: look up!
Crawlerknocking under the floor: look down!
Pentagramstand in it: 10 seconds of protection, then it recharges
Potionappears now and then: walk over it to see the ghosts for 15 seconds
Red demonthe guardian of the gate: 6 hits, takes 2 lives
The deadskeletons, werewolves and zombies from the graves: you can see them, 2 hits
Extra lifea glowing pumpkin appears halfway through each area

Hit a ghost with a spell before it reaches you. You have 3 pumpkins (lives).

Laptop: W A S D to walk · Shift to run · mouse to look · click or Space to cast · R to switch room · Esc to pause
VR: left stick to walk · hold grip to run · right stick to turn · point the wand and pull the trigger
